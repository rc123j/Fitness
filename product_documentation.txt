Core Product Flow
1. User installs app
2. Registers/login
3. Completes health onboarding
4. Generates screening report
5. Purchases personalized plan
6. Starts plan whenever convenient
7. Plan becomes active for 30 days from activation
8. User follows meals and reminders
9. Tracks progress daily
10. Receives rewards and gamification points
11. Re-checks weight after 30 days
12. New adaptive plan generated according to progress
13. Automatic expert consultation triggered if no progress observed

Important Business Logic
Personalized Plan Activation System
Flow
• Diet plans are NOT auto-started immediately after purchase.
• User can start the generated plan anytime according to their convenience.
• Once the user taps: "Start My Plan" the 30-day duration begins.

Plan Validity
• Every generated plan remains active for 30 days only.
• Timer should be visible on dashboard.
• Countdown indicator:
• Days left
• Completion percentage

Plan Renewal
• After 30 days:
• User must check updated weight
• User must update progress data
• New plan gets generated dynamically according to progress

Pricing Model (Updated)
Remove Traditional Membership Plans

• Monthly subscription

• Yearly subscription

Per Plan Pricing Model
