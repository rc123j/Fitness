# NutriSmart Pro — Screen-by-Screen Product Feature List
This document outlines the detailed screen-by-screen features, user options, business rules, and mathematical formulas of the NutriSmart Pro platform. It is written in plain bullet points for easy review by non-technical managers and stakeholders.

*Note: Sports Supplementation and Community Social Rooms have been excluded as requested.*

---

## 1. SPLASH SCREEN
*   **Purpose:** The app's entry point that establishes brand identity and handles automatic user routing.
*   **Key Features & Logic:**
    *   **Animated Branding:** Premium startup logo animation with loading indicators.
    *   **Auto-Login System:** Automatically checks if a user has a valid saved login session.
        *   If the user is logged in, they are immediately routed to the Home Dashboard.
        *   If the user is logged in as an Expert, they are routed to the Expert Consultation Portal.
        *   If the session is expired or new, the user is directed to the Welcome/Login screen.

## 4. STEP-BY-STEP ASSESSMENT ONBOARDING
*   **Purpose:** Collects key health data from the member to generate a personalized diet and lifestyle plan.
*   **Structure:** A linear, 10-step progress-tracked wizard that tailors itself based on the user's choices.
*   **Screen-by-Screen Breakdown:**
    *   **Step 1: Gender Selection:**
        *   Options: "Male" or "Female".
        *   Visuals: Displays corresponding gender-themed illustrations.
        *   Logic: Adjusts the app's accent colors (e.g., Male = Green/Blue theme, Female = Pink/Purple theme) and changes the BMR, TDEE, and Ideal Body Weight calculations.
    *   **Step 2: Age Input:**
        *   Interactivity: Interactive dial or slider.
        *   Visuals: Dynamically displays illustrations representing youth, middle age, or senior years based on gender and age range.
    *   **Step 3: Height Input:**
        *   Interactivity: Virtual ruler selector in centimeters (cm).
        *   Visuals: Renders a character standing against a height measuring tape.
    *   **Step 4: Weight Input:**
        *   Interactivity: Double slider to log current body weight and set target goal weight (in kg).
    *   **Step 5: Activity Level Selection:**
        *   Options:
            *   *Sedentary:* Little or no exercise.
            *   *Lightly Active:* Light exercise/sports 1–3 days a week.
            *   *Moderately Active:* Moderate exercise/sports 3–5 days a week.
            *   *Very Active:* Hard exercise/sports 6–7 days a week.
            *   *Extra Active:* Heavy physical job or training twice a day.
        *   Logic: Configures the physical activity level multiplier used to calculate the user's daily energy requirement.
    *   **Step 6: Goal Selection:**
        *   Options: "Fat Loss", "Muscle Gain", "Weight Gain", or "Maintenance".
        *   Logic: Dictates whether the final plan applies a calorie deficit or calorie surplus.
    *   **Step 7: Dietary Preference Selection:**
        *   Options: "Vegan", "Vegetarian", "Non-Veg", or "Eggetarian".
        *   Logic: Filters out disallowed ingredients and menu items during plan generation.
    *   **Step 8: Lifestyle Habits Input:**
        *   Options: Asks questions about sleeping hours, daily water intake goals, smoking habits, and alcohol consumption frequency.
    *   **Step 9: Health Profile Selection:**
        *   Options: Allows selecting existing medical conditions (e.g., "Diabetes", "Hypertension", "PCOS", "Thyroid").
        *   Logic:
            *   *Gender Restriction:* PCOS option is dynamically hidden/ignored if "Male" was selected in Step 1.
            *   *Dietary Safety:* Disallows specific foods during meal generation that might negatively affect listed conditions.
    *   **Step 10: Optional Metrics Input:**
        *   Options: Allows inputs for chest, waist, hips, and body fat percentage measurements (in inches/percentage).

## 5. SCREENING REPORT SCREEN (STEP 11)
*   **Purpose:** Summarizes the user's physical diagnostics immediately after finishing the onboarding.
*   **Calculations & Formulas:**
    *   **BMI (Body Mass Index):**
        *   *Formula:* `Weight (kg) / (Height (m) * Height (m))`
        *   *Categories:* Renders on a color-coded gauge (Underweight < 18.5, Normal 18.5–22.9, Overweight 23–24.9, Obese >= 25).
    *   **BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate):**
        *   *Formula (Mifflin-St Jeor):*
            *   Male: `10 * Weight (kg) + 6.25 * Height (cm) - 5 * Age (years) + 5`
            *   Female: `10 * Weight (kg) + 6.25 * Height (cm) - 5 * Age (years) - 161`
    *   **Ideal Body Weight (IBW):**
        *   *Formula (Devine Method):*
            *   Male: `Height (cm) - 100`
            *   Female: `Height (cm) - 105`
    *   **%IBW (Ideal Weight Ratio):**
        *   *Formula:* `(Current Weight / Ideal Body Weight) * 100`
    *   **TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure):**
        *   *Formula:* `BMR * Activity Multiplier`
        *   *Multipliers:*
            *   *Sedentary:* Male = 1.3 | Female = 1.3
            *   *Lightly Active:* Male = 1.6 | Female = 1.5
            *   *Moderately Active:* Male = 1.7 | Female = 1.6
            *   *Very Active:* Male = 2.1 | Female = 1.9
            *   *Extra Active:* Male = 2.4 | Female = 2.2
    *   **Daily Target Calories:**
        *   *Fat Loss:* `TDEE - 500 kcal` (Deficit)
        *   *Weight Gain / Muscle Gain:* `TDEE + 300 to 400 kcal` (Surplus)
        *   *Maintenance:* `TDEE`
    *   **Daily Macronutrient Split Targets:**
        *   *Carbohydrates:* 45% of Daily Target Calories (split into grams: `calories * 0.45 / 4`)
        *   *Fats:* 25% of Daily Target Calories (split into grams: `calories * 0.25 / 9`)
        *   *Protein:* Fixed daily intake target calculated as `Ideal Body Weight (IBW) * 1.2` in grams.
*   **Interactive Visual Elements:**
    *   **BMI Gauge:** An arc chart displaying the user's exact classification.
    *   **Metrics Summary Cards:** Grid displaying BMR, IBW, TDEE, and %IBW values.
    *   **Recommended Macros Chart:** Shows split targets for Protein, Carbs, and Fats.
    *   **Scientific Rationale Text:** Dynamic summary explaining the recommended calorie count and workout target for their chosen goal.

## 7. HOME DASHBOARD
*   **Purpose:** The central landing screen of the mobile application showing today's diet plan and macro statistics.
*   **Key Features & Layout:**
    *   **Top Navigation Header:** Displays user's first name, current profile photo, notifications badge, and a shortcut to the FitCoins wallet.
    *   **Swiggy-Style Tab Switcher:** Swaps between "Meal" and "Workout" tabs.
        *   *Meal Tab:* Renders active daily content (macros, timeline, promotions).
        *   *Workout Tab:* Currently displays a "Coming Soon" splash graphic.
    *   **The 3 Core Macro Progress Cards:** Renders three cards: Protein, Carbohydrates, and Fats.
        *   Displays: Consumed grams vs Target grams (e.g., "40g / 120g").
        *   Visuals: Features progress bars that fill up as the user completes meals throughout the day.
    *   **Active Plan Card (Glassmorphic Card):**
        *   Displays: Plan name, plan pace message, days remaining (e.g., "Day 12 of 30"), and progress bar.
        *   Navigation: Tapping takes the user to the Detailed Progress screen.
    *   **Today's Meal Timeline Quick-View:** Displays a preview of meal slots (Breakfast, Mid-Meal, Lunch, etc.) with calorie counts. Tapping navigates to the main Meal Planner screen.
    *   **Sticky Search Bar:** Appears at the top of the screen when scrolling down to enable easy food searches.
    *   **Offer/Unlock Subscriptions Card:** Appears for users with expired or unpurchased plans, opening a pricing model bottom sheet showing Monthly and Annual plan selections.

## 8. SMART MEAL PLANNER SCREEN
*   **Purpose:** Displays the full, detailed meal list for any selected calendar day and manages meal logs.
*   **Key Features & Conditions:**
    *   **Weekly Calendar Picker:** Shows dates for the current week, allowing user to swipe back and forth.
        *   *Lock Rule:* Clicking future dates, or dates prior to the active plan's start date, restricts the user to a "Meal Locked" screen.
    *   **Insight Switcher Tabs (Conditional on Locked State):**
        *   If the selected date is locked: Only the "Meal" tab is visible.
        *   If the selected date is active: Displays multiple helpful tabs:
            *   *Meal Tab:* Displays the daily recommended meal cards.
            *   *Macros Tab:* Charts the exact target vs consumed carbs, protein, and fats.
            *   *Deficiency Tab:* (Shown if deficiencies are registered) Highlights priority vitamins/minerals.
            *   *Anthropometric Tab:* Shows user measurements (BMI, BMR, TDEE).
            *   *Disease Guidance Tab:* (Shown if medical conditions exist) Details specific foods to eat, foods to avoid, and precautions.
            *   *Fiber & Protein Tab:* Details fiber foods and protein powder schedules.
            *   *Advice Tab:* General health tips.
    *   **Individual Meal Cards:** Represents Breakfast, Mid-Meal, Lunch, Evening Snack, Dinner, and Water Intake.
        *   *Food Item Listings:* Details recommended food names, portions, and calories (kcal).
        *   *Swapping/Alternatives:* Lists alternative food swap suggestions (e.g., "Swap: Oats OR Egg whites").
    *   **"Mark as Complete" / Meal Logging Logic:**
        *   *Today's Logic:* Users can click "Mark as Complete" once the meal's scheduled time has arrived.
        *   *Too-Early Logic:* If the time of day has not arrived, the button is locked, displaying "Unlocks at [Time]" (e.g., "Unlocks 1:00 PM"). Tapping it displays "You can mark this meal from [Time]."
        *   *Past Dates Logic:* Meals on past days are closed. Tapping shows "This day's meal log is closed — you can only mark today's meals."
        *   *Future Dates Logic:* Future meals are locked. Tapping shows "Meals can only be marked on the day itself."
        *   *Undo Logging:* Today's marked meals can be clicked again to unmark ("Marked as Eaten" transitions back to "Mark as Complete").
        *   *Dynamic Update:* Completing a meal instantly updates dashboard macros.

## 9. PROGRESS & ANALYTICS VIEWS
*   **Purpose:** Tracks long-term consistency, weight changes, and overall compliance.
*   **Key Features & Options:**
    *   **Discipline Adherence Banner:** Displays overall consistency rate (e.g., "92% Meal Compliance").
    *   **Journey Progress Tracker:** Shows Day X of 30 progress bar and remaining days.
    *   **Analytics Tab Switcher:**
        *   *Today Tab:* Shows total daily consumed calories vs target calories, macro progress rings, active logging streak count, and daily health tip.
        *   *Weight Tab:* Displays starting snapshots, weight difference stats, and the Weight History Graph.
    *   **Estimated Weight Tracking Logic (No Scales Integration):**
        *   *Important Condition:* The app does not sync with smart scales or allow manual weight logging.
        *   *Algorithm:* Bodyweight is estimated based on the calorie deficit or surplus logged.
        *   *Formula:* The system logs calories from eaten meals and compares it against TDEE. Every 7,700 kcal deficit registers as 1 kg of estimated fat loss; every 7,700 kcal surplus registers as 1 kg of estimated fat gain.
    *   **Starting Snapshot Card:** Archive of baseline onboarding data (BMI, BMR, TDEE, IBW).

## 10. PROGRESS PHOTOS (TRANSFORMATION GALLERY)
*   **Purpose:** Allows users to visually compare their bodies at specific milestones.
*   **Milestone Milestones & Lock Logic:**
    *   **Milestone Slots:** Day 1, Week 1 (Day 7), Week 2 (Day 14), Week 3 (Day 21), Day 30 (Day 28).
    *   **Lock Rules:** Milestone slots are locked until the user's active plan day reaches or passes the threshold. Future milestone cards display a lock icon.
    *   **Photo Actions:**
        *   Tapping a locked card opens a bottom sheet explanation (e.g., "Week 2 Photo Locked until Day 14").
        *   Tapping an unlocked empty card opens the phone camera or photo gallery to upload a photo.
        *   Tapping an uploaded slot displays the photo in full screen with an "Edit/Replace Photo" option.

*   **Key Features:**

## 13. EXPERT AVAILABILITY & SLOTS MANAGEMENT (EXPERT/ADMIN SIDE)
*   **Purpose:** Allows consultants/admins to customize their daily calendar availability.
*   **Key Features & Validation Rules:**
    *   **Calendar Timeline Select:** Table Calendar to review configured dates.
    *   **Availability List:** Shows slots configured for the selected date. Only slots that remain "AVAILABLE" (unbooked) can be deleted by clicking the "x" button.
    *   **Add Availability Slot Dialog:**
        *   *Auto-Suggestion:* Smartly pre-fills start and end times based on the last configured slot for that day (defaulting to 1-hour durations).
        *   *Time Verification:* Prevents adding slots where the end time is before the start time.
        *   *Overlapping Overlap Check:* Compares start/end times against existing slots. If a slot overlaps, it blocks saving and triggers a "Time Conflict" warning message.

## 14. EXPERT CONSULTATION PORTAL (EXPERT/ADMIN SIDE)
*   **Purpose:** The central control dashboard for experts to review and conduct member sessions.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Active Appointments List:** Shows PENDING and APPROVED client appointments.
    *   **Status Indicators:** Displays color-coded badges for status (PENDING = Yellow, APPROVED = Green, COMPLETED = Blue, CANCELLED/REJECTED = Red).
    *   **Consultant Actions:**
        *   *Pending Appointments:* Single-tap buttons to "Approve" or "Reject" requests.
        *   *Approved Appointments:*
            *   *Message Button:* Launches text chat with the client.
            *   *Add Notes / Complete Button:* Opens a dialog for the expert to type health recommendations, workout tips, and complete/save the session.
            *   *Start Call Button (Agora Integration):* Unlocks 10 minutes prior to start time. Displays "Session Ended" once expired, or "Available at [Time]" if clicked too early.
